    How to play

    Guess the hidden word. After every guess, each tile changes color:

    CRANE

    Green: the letter is in the word and in the right spot.

    CRANE

    Yellow: the letter is in the word, but in the wrong spot.

    CRANE

    Gray: the letter is not in the word.

    Good to know

    • You get letters + 1 tries, with 4, 5 or 6 letters. Every guess must be a real word.
    • Click a box in your row to fix just that letter.
    • Wins earn XP, and faster wins earn more. Your streak grows with every win.
    • Hint shows a letter, then the meaning, for 25% less XP each. Skip counts as a loss.
    • Hard mode makes you reuse revealed hints. Blind mode hides past letters, so only colors stay. Both earn more XP.
    • Try Speedrun for timed runs on random words. No XP, just records and badges.
    • Solve at least one word a day to build a daily streak and unlock board themes.
    • Join the Leaderboard with a unique name to compete.

    About wordbox

    wordbox is an independent, fan-made word game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to The New York Times Company or Wordle. Wordle is a trademark of The New York Times Company, and all other names belong to their owners.

    The game uses its own code and design. The 5-letter answers and guesses use the word lists from hello wordl by Lynn (@chordbug). For 4 and 6 letters, answers come from a common-words list and guesses are checked against the public-domain ENABLE word list. Definitions come from Wiktionary.

    Want the word of the day? Play the original Wordle on The New York Times Games site, then come back here for as many extra rounds as you like.

    Your progress is saved in your browser. If you join the leaderboard, your name and scores are stored online.
